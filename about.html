Our Story

Founded with a vision to foster innovation and rural development, SIIF SLIET stands as a dedicated platform nurturing grassroots entrepreneurs, student innovators, and rural technology advancements.

What started as a focused initiative within SLIET has evolved into a thriving center of innovation, supporting over 200+ projects and startups, empowering communities, and driving sustainable development through technology and collaboration.

Mission

Message from Our Director

"At SIIF SLIET, we believe innovation has the power to transform lives, especially in rural communities. Our goal is to empower students, entrepreneurs, and innovators to develop sustainable solutions that address real-world challenges.

Beyond technology, we focus on building a culture of creativity, collaboration, and ethical leadership. Through our dedicated mentorship and supportive environment, we encourage every innovator to think boldly and work towards meaningful impact.

I invite you to explore SIIF SLIET and become part of our journey in shaping a self-reliant, innovative India."
